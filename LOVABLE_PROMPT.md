## What the product is

GTA 6 Clipper is a self-hosted desktop tool that turns long videos and live streams into captioned, ready-to-post vertical clips (9:16). The user pastes a YouTube, Twitch or Kick link (or uploads a file), and the software uses AI to find the moments worth clipping, cuts them to vertical, adds word-by-word captions in three styles, and can post to connected accounts.

Key capabilities to mention accurately:
- Clips from live streams while they are running, and from long recorded videos
- AI finds the best moments; without an AI key it picks loud moments with speech
- Three caption styles (Bounce, Clean, Hype)
- A "Reframe" editor: crop/zoom/pan cutouts, optional background image that fills empty bars, adjustable split-screen divider, editable caption size and position
- Two divided video layouts: gameplay top / person bottom and the reverse
- Runs on the buyer's own computer with Python and ffmpeg. No subscription, no monthly fee. One-time purchase of ₹399.

## Who it is for and why they want it

The audience is people who want to make money online by clipping GTA 6 gameplay. The business model: they join a clipping platform such as Content Rewards or Vyro, apply to a streamer's GTA 6 clipping campaign, take the stream or video link, run it through GTA 6 Clipper to get captioned clips, then post those clips on TikTok / Instagram / YouTube Shorts. They are paid roughly $1-5 per 1,000 views depending on the campaign. No followers needed because payment is per view, not per follower. GTA 6 is the biggest game release in over a decade with enormous view demand, which is why GTA 6 clips get attention.

**Positioning: sell the dream, backed by facts.** The visitor should feel the earning potential immediately. The headline and the monthly number should be the first thing they see.
