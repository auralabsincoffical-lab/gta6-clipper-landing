## GTA 6 Clipper

An AI-powered desktop app that turns long videos and live streams into captioned, vertical clips, ready to post.

Paste a YouTube, Twitch or Kick link, or upload a video file. GTA 6 Clipper's AI watches the footage, finds the moments worth keeping, cuts them into 9:16 shorts and writes word-by-word captions. A full editor lets you fine-tune every clip before you export. Everything runs on your own computer, so your files and settings stay with you.

### The AI engine

- **AI clip scout.** The software transcribes the audio and asks a chat model which moments would hold a stranger's attention. It scores each moment and suggests a hook, title and caption. Without an AI key, it falls back to picking the loudest moments that contain speech.
- **AI caption writing.** Word-by-word captions in three styles (Bounce, Clean and Hype), with romanisation for non-English speech. Position and size are editable.
- **Bring your own model.** Works with Gemini, OpenAI, Grok, Groq, OpenRouter, Claude, Ollama, or any service that speaks the OpenAI chat format. You connect the key and pay that provider directly. Speech to text runs locally, so audio never leaves your machine.

### What the app does

- **Clip from any source.** Paste a link to a long video or a live stream, or upload a local file. The AI finds the best moments and produces finished vertical clips.
- **Live clipping.** Record a stream into a rolling buffer and cut highlights while the stream is still running, using loudness, chat activity and the transcript.
- **Reframe editor.** Open any finished clip and crop it with one or two cutouts. Resize and move each cutout, zoom and pan, and stack two cutouts into a split-screen layout with a draggable divider. Swap which cutout sits on top.
- **Background fill.** When zooming out leaves empty bars, add an optional image that fills the space automatically. Use one image for the whole frame or separate images for the top and bottom sections.
- **Divided layouts.** Two ready-made arrangements: gameplay on top with the person below, or the person on top with gameplay below. Both are fully adjustable in the editor.
- **Creator workspaces.** Manage channels, clip settings, posting rules and an activity log for each creator you clip.
- **Posting.** Queue or auto-post finished clips to connected YouTube and Instagram accounts, with spacing and daily caps.

### Requirements

- Python 3.10 or newer
- ffmpeg installed on your system
- A one-time speech-model download of about 480 MB on first setup
- Windows, macOS or Linux
- An AI key is optional; without one the software still clips using loudness and speech detection
