Straight answers

Do I need followers or a big channel?

Clipping campaigns pay for the views each clip gets. Follower count is irrelevant, so a brand new account can earn if a clip takes off.

Is clipping someone else's stream legal?

Only clip footage the campaign brief explicitly allows. GTA 6 Clipper gives you the tool; the right to the footage comes from the campaign. When in doubt, read the brief or the platform's help centre before posting.

Are these earnings guaranteed?

No. The calculator is an illustration using published platform rates. Actual earnings depend on campaign terms, approvals, view counts, per-clip caps and fees. Treat clipping as performance-based income, not a salary.

Do I need an expensive PC?

No. Python and ffmpeg do the work on a normal computer. The speech model is a one-time download of about 480 MB. A faster machine only makes renders quicker.

Is this still worth doing now that the game is out?

Yes. GTA 6 campaigns keep running, and new gaming campaigns open daily on both platforms. The skill of finding and cutting good moments keeps paying long after the launch buzz fades.

What happens after I pay?

You get the downloadable AI app and a plain-English setup guide. You install it on your own computer, add your AI key to activate the smart clip scout and caption writing, and start clipping.
